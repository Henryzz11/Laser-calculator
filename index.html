D=4 λ fπ MFD

Derivation

For an ideal Gaussian mode with M2 = 1, the mode field radius is

w0=MFD2
θ=λπ w0
D=2 f θ=4 λ fπ MFD

The factor 4 comes from converting radius to diameter twice: MFD = 2w0 and D = 2w.

Notes

f
Lens focal length

Input beam diameter, or the effective clear aperture when the lens clips the beam
f/# < 4
Fast focus; plano-convex spherical aberration can become a serious limitation
f/# 4-8
Moderate focus; check spherical aberration, especially for fiber coupling or small spots
f/# > 8
Gentler focus; usually lower aberration if the beam fill is modest, but spot size is larger

Practical warning

If a plano-convex lens is used at low f-number, spherical aberration can become significant. An aspheric lens or microscope objective usually improves small-f/# focusing.

If the beam diameter approaches the clear aperture, consider both spherical aberration and clipping carefully.
